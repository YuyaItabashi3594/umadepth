### SSR Mr.CB
One of the best Wit card,and I prefer this to FineMo so I use Mr.CB and NN for all time.
If your oshi is End Closer you must have MLB. Otherwise you should have 1LB at least. 3LB is better.
Comparing to FineMo,this card has:

- Homestretch Haste and Tail(and Corner Recovery sometimes)
- all chain events give friendship gauge
- slightly higher Prio
and cons are:
- worse Friendship training than FineMo
- 5% Race Bonus
- 5% TE for other trainings
Some say this card will be fallen when ramonu comes,but this card is still top tier during entire Trackblazer and GrandLive.
